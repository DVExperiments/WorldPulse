DATA & TRANSPARENCY

What you're actually seeing

WorldPulse visualizes locations mentioned in news coverage monitored by GDELT. Markers are geographic mentions, not proof that an event occurred at the exact pin and not the publisher's location.

Each category queries GDELT GEO 2.0's rolling 24-hour window. Marker size reflects volume of matched location mentions. Category color describes the query theme—it is not a machine-verified classification of each linked story. One story can be counted in multiple places or categories.

Coverage is incomplete. GDELT has source, language, location-extraction and recency biases. The up-to-five links per location are examples, not an exhaustive article list. Feed refreshes are scheduled on GitHub Actions but may be delayed or fail.

Translation: WorldPulse attempts on-device translation when the browser's Translator API supports it, otherwise keeps the original title. Source article links remain unchanged. Social: no posts are mapped unless a location-verified feed is supplied. Coordinates on posts must be consented to and independently appropriate to publish.

No account, tracking pixel, or location permission is needed. The globe texture and JavaScript load from external CDNs.
