DATA & TRANSPARENCY

How WorldPulse measures coverage

WorldPulse v1.4A.2 samples Google News RSS queries and independent publisher RSS feeds across multiple regions and languages, across eleven topics including war and conflict. It looks for place names in headlines first and descriptions second; neither proves where an event happened. Country pins are approximate country anchors, not incident coordinates.

Counts are deduplicated mapped articles within these samples, not a complete count of everything reported worldwide. Google News articles inherit their query category; direct feeds use multilingual keyword heuristics. Categories may be imperfect and overlapping in reality. Each marker's three-hour velocity compares matching articles published in the latest three hours with the preceding three hours. This is a directional sample and can change as RSS rankings and queries change.

Repeated coverage of an event by distinct sources can remain as separate articles. Filtering and geocoding are heuristic and imperfect. Matching favors place names included in the gazetteer; additional multilingual feeds do not guarantee multilingual geographic recognition. Click a linked source to verify the article. A category's rising count does not establish an increase in violence or verified incident frequency.

Translation: WorldPulse attempts on-device translation when the browser's Translator API supports it, otherwise keeps the original title. Source article links remain unchanged. Social: no posts are mapped unless a location-verified feed is supplied. Coordinates on posts must be consented to and independently appropriate to publish.

No account, tracking pixel, or location permission is needed. The globe texture and JavaScript load from external CDNs.
